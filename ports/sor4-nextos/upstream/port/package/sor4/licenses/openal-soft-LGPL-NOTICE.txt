OpenAL Soft 1.21.1 notice
========================

This package includes an unmodified, dynamically linked AArch64
`libopenal.so.1` built from OpenAL Soft 1.21.1.

Upstream project: https://openal-soft.org/
Corresponding source tag: https://github.com/kcat/openal-soft/tree/1.21.1
Corresponding source archive:
https://github.com/kcat/openal-soft/archive/refs/tags/1.21.1.tar.gz
License: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later.
Complete license text: LGPL-2.1-or-later.txt

The library is dynamically loaded and may be replaced with a compatible build.
The bundled build provides PulseAudio and ALSA backends selected automatically
through `alsoft.conf`; the launcher does not force an SDL audio backend.
