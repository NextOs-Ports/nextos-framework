mpg123 notice
=============

The package bundles an AArch64 fallback copy of libmpg123. A compatible
firmware-provided library remains preferred by the launcher.

mpg123 is licensed under LGPL-2.1-or-later. The complete license text is in
`LGPL-2.1-or-later.txt`. Project information is available from
https://www.mpg123.de/.
