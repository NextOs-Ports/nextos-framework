The APK, native Android libraries, assemblies, artwork, music and other game
content remain the property of their respective owners. This package accepts
compatible owner-supplied AArch64 data for package com.pid.scourgebringer. The
physically validated layouts are APK 1.61.16 with the classic assembly store
and APK 1.61 b19 with the assembly blob DSO. A bounded flexible recipe also
permits b17, b18 and other compatible 1.61.x inputs when they retain the
required package, ABI, core content, 12 languages and libraries. NXExtract
checks and extracts those data only on the owner's device.
