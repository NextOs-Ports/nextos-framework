emulated AArch64 ABI and low-glibc gates pass. The exact ELF above passed a
physical glibc 2.41 KMSDRM launch through MainActivity, FMOD/ALSA, controller,
READY 9/9 and rendered swaps. Root-menu left/right retained visible selection,
vertical navigation still selected Settings, horizontal input worked inside
Settings, and full-range -32768/32767 horizontal movement was delivered and
visually confirmed during gameplay. This is a regression proof only;
acceptance of the old-glibc fix on glibc 2.30 remains pending.
