# NXExtract 1.2.15

NXExtract 1.2.15 splits the rule checked on the freshly extracted payload from
the rule checked on the final result after hooks, and changes nothing else: the
setup screen, its pixels, the terminal-result schema and the immutable 1.2.9 UI
bytes are unchanged.

## Why

Until now the same `validate` block of an `extract` item was checked twice —
once on the payload as extracted, and again on the result after any hook ran.
That is harmless while hooks do not touch the payload. When a hook legitimately
changes it — recompressing a texture, downmixing audio — no single rule
describes both states, so the recipe has to widen until it fits both.

The cost is not cosmetic. A widened rule also accepts a stage interrupted *in
the middle* of the transformation, part original and part converted, which is
precisely the state it should reject.

## What changed

An `extract` item accepts two optional members:

- `source_validate` — checked on the freshly extracted payload, before hooks;
- `output_validate` — checked on the final result, after hooks.

Each side can now declare exact sizes and fingerprints, and an interrupted
stage satisfies neither.

Every source-side phase uses the source rule: candidate choice inside the APK,
tree planning, copy and resume. Those were places where the output rule would
otherwise be checked against a payload that had not been transformed yet.

## Compatibility

Purely additive. An item that declares only `validate` behaves exactly as
before on both sides, byte for byte. Recipes do not need to change.

Published ports are not migrated: 1.2.14 remains a supported engine, recorded
with its own identity in `framework/nxrelease/nxextract-engines-v1.json`, and a
package may keep shipping it until its port opts in.
