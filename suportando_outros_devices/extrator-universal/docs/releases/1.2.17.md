# NXExtract 1.2.17

NXExtract 1.2.17 makes preparation hooks transactional, by opt-in, and changes
nothing else: the setup screen, the separately pinned immutable 1.2.16 UI, the
terminal-result schema and the NXE#### format are unchanged. Hooks that do not
declare the new member behave byte-identically to before.

## Why

The executor has always published the *stage* transactionally, but the outputs
of the hook itself were not atomic. A preparator that replaces several files
one by one — the motivating case is Brotato's shader preparation, 32 files —
could be interrupted between targets and leave the stage as a mix of original
and transformed files. Resuming that stage could not be trusted: the hook had
no way to know how much had already been applied, and its own pristine-input
validation then failed forever until the stage was discarded by hand.

## What changed

- An additive boolean hook member, `"transactional": true`. The engine then
  provides an empty shadow workspace in `NXEXTRACT_HOOK_SHADOW`; the hook
  writes **every** output there, mirroring stage-relative paths, and never
  touches the validated stage inputs.
- After the hook exits 0 the engine proves the checkpoint inputs unchanged
  (SHA-256 before/after the run), enumerates and fsyncs the shadow outputs,
  validates the checkpoint on the shadow+stage overlay, and seals a journal
  with per-output SHA-256, counters and an integral fingerprint — all before
  the first byte reaches the stage.
- Publication is one atomic rename per target, with directory `fsync`, and
  every target is verified before the first rename. Interruption before the
  seal discards the temporaries; interruption after the seal rolls forward
  deterministically from the journal. A journal whose shadow was already
  consumed — the resumed extraction restores replaced targets from the
  source — is discarded and the hook reruns over pristine data. A partially
  prepared stage is never adopted.
- Transactional hooks run unbuffered (`PYTHONUNBUFFERED=1`) and a hook failure
  carries the last detail line, sanitized (no absolute paths, no owner
  container names), into the summary and the terminal error.

## Proof

`tests/test_hook_transaction.py` runs the real extractor and injects the P6
item-5 faults: `SIGKILL` of the whole extractor after each target, a simulated
`ENOSPC` and a validation failure. In every scenario the retry of the same
container ends at the same fingerprint, with no `.nxpart`, no extra target and
no change to the previously published live payload. The port-level pattern was
proven first in Brotato's preparator, which now uses the same
shadow-and-journal transaction standalone and produces its outputs in the
engine shadow when `NXEXTRACT_HOOK_SHADOW` is present.

## Compatibility

- Recipes without `transactional` are untouched; the 77-test regression suite
  and the phase-validation gate pass unchanged.
- The UI set stays the immutable 1.2.16 release: no binary was rebuilt, the
  release manifest and pixel goldens are untouched.
- 1.2.16 moves to `supported` in the engine-identity registry; published ports
  keep their pinned engine until they migrate by opt-in. No port is migrated
  automatically.
